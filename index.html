Fledge

Three habits. Done for you. Then they fly.

The habit tracker that lets go. Fledge tracks three habits at a time, fills them in from Apple Health, and graduates each one once it sticks, about 66 days on average.

  • Done for you. Walking, exercise, sleep, mindful minutes, water and daylight complete themselves from Apple Health.
  • A head start. See your own last 30 days before you begin, and pick a goal you can win.
  • Then they fly. From day 30, a habit you keep on most days can graduate and leave your list.
  • Private. No account. Your Health data stays on your device.

Fledge is coming to the App Store for iPhone and iPad.
