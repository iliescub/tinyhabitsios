Fledge Pro

  • Fledge Pro is offered as a yearly or monthly auto-renewing subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase.
  • The yearly plan may include a free trial. If it does, you won't be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel before then without paying.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase (or when the free trial ends).
  • A subscription renews automatically unless it's turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or from Fledge's Settings. Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you buy a subscription.
  • Restore earlier purchases in Fledge › Settings › About › Restore purchases.

Health information

Fledge helps you build habits. It isn't a medical device and doesn't give medical advice. Talk to a doctor before changing your exercise, sleep or diet, especially if you have a health condition.
