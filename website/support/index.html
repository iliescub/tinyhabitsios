Fledge support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Need help? Open an issue on GitHub with your device, iOS version and what you expected to happen. Please don't include health data.

Apple Health

My Health habit stays at zero

Check access in the Health app › your profile › Apps › Fledge, and turn on the data types your habit uses. Apple doesn't tell apps when read access is off, so Fledge can only see "no data". Some types need an Apple Watch (exercise minutes, stand hours) or a sleep tracker.

Values update late

Fledge refreshes when you open it, and Apple wakes it in the background about once an hour for steps. Widgets show when Health last updated. If your Watch syncs late, last night's sleep can appear in the morning.

What is the head start?

When you add a Health habit, Fledge reads your last 30 days so you can pick a realistic goal. Those days show faded on the habit's history as "Before Fledge". They don't count toward streaks or graduation.

Habits

Why only three?

Focus works. When a habit sticks, it graduates and frees its slot. You can also pause a habit to make room.

When can a habit fly?

From day 30, once you've kept it on at least 80% of the last 30 days. Fledge shows the day it's on track to fly. Graduating is always your choice.

What happens after a habit flies?

Health habits keep tracking quietly on the Flown shelf. If one drops below half of the last 14 days, Fledge nudges you once and offers to bring it back.

Rest days

Daily habits get two rest days a month that don't break your streak. Habits set to a few days a week can rest whenever they like.

The day ends at 3 AM

So late nights count for the right day. Change it in Settings › Day ends at.

Widgets, Siri and Control Center

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget and choose Fledge. Tap a ring on a widget to check in a tap-to-log habit. Ask Siri "How are my habits in Fledge".

Fledge Pro

Restore or manage a purchase

Settings › About › Restore purchases. To cancel, use Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your device.

What's free?

Three habits, graduation and the small widget are free, with one habit filled in from Apple Health. Pro lets all three fill in by themselves and adds every widget size, Lock Screen widgets, app icons and CSV export.

Your data

Everything stays on your device; see the privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
