Yearling

One resolution. Kept by December.

The New Year's resolution app that ends with a kept resolution. Make one resolution. Yearling turns it into up to three small habits with a plan, fills them in from Apple Health, and lets each one fly once it sticks. When they all have, your resolution is kept.

  • A plan, not a wish. Every habit gets an "after X" plan: "After lunch, walk 6,000 steps."
  • Start, don't just stop. Wrote "stop scrolling in bed"? Yearling suggests something to start instead.
  • Done for you. Walking, exercise, sleep, mindful minutes, water and daylight complete themselves from Apple Health.
  • Fresh starts, not failures. A quiet week means Monday is a fresh start, never a broken streak.
  • Private. No account. Your Health data, your resolution and your letter stay on your device.

Yearling is coming to the App Store for iPhone and iPad.
