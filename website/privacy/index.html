Privacy policy

No account. No tracking. Your data stays on your device.

Effective 14 October 2026

In short: Fledge has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your habits and anything Fledge reads from Apple Health stay on your device. We can't see them.

What Fledge stores

Your habits (names, icons, goals, reminders), one entry per habit per day (whether it was done, and the day's value), and your settings. They are kept on your iPhone or iPad, in storage shared only with Fledge's own widgets. Fledge has no servers and uploads nothing.

This version doesn't use iCloud. If iCloud sync is added later, it will sync only habit definitions and done/skipped marks, never values read from Apple Health, and this policy will be updated first.

Apple Health

Fledge only reads from Apple Health, and only the types needed for the habits you add. It never writes to Apple Health. Depending on your habits, it may ask to read:

  • Steps, walking and running distance, flights climbed
  • Exercise minutes, workouts, stand hours
  • Mindful minutes, sleep
  • Water, protein
  • Time in daylight

Fledge reads daily totals to fill in your habits, to show your last 30 days when you add a habit (the "head start"), and to check how flown habits are holding up. Health data never leaves your device, is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never sold or shared with anyone. You can turn access off at any time in the Health app › your profile › Apps › Fledge.

Notifications

Reminders and the occasional note about a flown habit are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent from a server.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. Fledge only learns whether Fledge Pro is active; it never sees your payment details.

Analytics and crash reports

Fledge contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. If you share crash reports with developers in your device's settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash logs to us.

Your choices

  • Export your history as a CSV file in Settings (Fledge Pro).
  • Delete a habit and its history in the habit's menu, or delete the app to remove everything.
  • Revoke Apple Health access in the Health app at any time.

Children

Fledge isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub, or see Support.
