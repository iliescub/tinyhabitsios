Fledge 1.0 · roadmap and positioning review · 14 Oct 2026

The habit tracker that lets go

I checked the roadmap against what the market already offers. Two of the three things we planned to lead with are already done by others. One isn't, and it has research behind it: graduation. Lead with it, and add only the two small features that make it true and visible on day one.

DifferentiatorHabits graduate. Fledge is built to make itself unnecessary for each habit, and to let go once a habit sticks.
New featuresTwo, about 3 days in total, both before the 29 Nov freeze: a slip check for flown habits (the app already promises it) and a Health head start on day one.
Not addingAI coach, social features, app blocking, more slots, an Apple Watch app in 1.0. Each one dilutes the story or misses the date.
AdvertisingChange the App Store subtitle, screenshots and campaign line to lead with graduation. "Done for you" becomes the proof, no longer the headline.

2 · Why graduation is the story

Most habit apps want you forever. Fledge wants your habits to fly.

That line isn't just a slogan; three research findings support it. They also give press and creators something to write about beyond "another habit tracker".

66 daysThe average time to automaticity in Lally et al. (UCL), ranging from 18 to an estimated 254 days. One missed day caused only a small, short dip.
DependencyRenfree et al. (CHI 2016): reminders and streaks help people repeat a behaviour but build reliance on the app. People who abandon the app often drop the behaviour too.
Cues, not pingsA CHI 2019 paper: relying on reminders supported repetition but held back habit formation. Event-based cues increased automaticity.

This is why "done for you" belongs under graduation, not beside it. Auto-completion means fewer taps and fewer reminders, so the app stays out of the way while the habit forms. Three slots give each habit room until it can leave. Every feature serves one promise.

4 · Recommended scope change

Add two features, about 3 days, before 29 Nov

P0

GRAD-3 · Slip check for flown habits · 1 day

When a flown Health habit falls below 50% over 14 days (HoldingRate.slipping, already built and tested), send one gentle notification: "Walk has slipped a little. Bring it back to the nest for a while?" It opens the Flown shelf.

  • Once per slip: nothing more until the habit is back above 80%. Never for tap-to-log graduates, which nobody logs any more.
  • Add it to ReminderPlan (HabitCore, tested) and let Reminders.reconcile schedule it.
  • This keeps the safety net the app already advertises. Without it, the line is a false promise, which matters for reviews.
P0

HEAD-1 · Health head start · 2 days

When someone adds a Health habit, Fledge reads their last 30 days and shows them in setup: "You've averaged 5,400 steps. 6,000 was met on 12 of the last 30 days." It also suggests a goal just above their baseline, small enough to win.

  • If the goal was already met on 24 or more of the last 30 days (80%, the graduation bar), say so: "Walk is already a habit for you. Pick a bigger goal, or choose something new." This is the graduation idea showing up on day one, and no competitor we found has it.
  • The heatmap starts filled with those 30 days, marked "before Fledge", so day one isn't an empty grid. Graduation still counts from the start date; the 30-day minimum doesn't change.
  • HealthReader.value(for:on:) already reads one day, so this needs a 30-day loop and a pure BaselineModel in HabitCore with tests. Everything is read on the device and nothing is stored beyond the day logs.
P1

GRAD-4 · Flight forecast · half a day, only if time allows

On the detail screen: "On track to fly around 12 Nov", from the start date plus the 30-day rule and the current rate. A date to look forward to makes the 30 days feel shorter, and it's a good line for creators.

Considered and left out of 1.0

IdeaWhy not now
AI coach or AI habit suggestionsA crowded claim in 2026, it conflicts with "Private by default" and "Native and quiet", and it adds cost. The head start gives personal advice from the user's own data without AI.
Social and accountability partnersA non-goal in the spec. It needs accounts and a backend, and shared trackers can turn a bad week into public guilt.
App blocking until habits are doneThe opposite of "No guilt" and of graduation. It also needs the Family Controls entitlement, which Apple has to approve.
More than three habits for ProPrinciple 1. Three only works with graduation; selling more slots would undo the story.
Apple Watch appKeep it in 1.1 as planned. Watch Health data already completes habits without one.
AndroidA non-goal in the spec. HealthKit and widgets are the product.

Schedule: the freeze is 29 Nov and today is 14 Oct. The two P0 features fit in the week of 20 Oct, alongside the device tests (HK-5, WID-5, IPAD-2). If they slip, cut GRAD-4 first, then the "before Fledge" heatmap fill. Never cut GRAD-3; drop the promise from the copy instead.

6 · Advertising plan

One story for every channel, from now to mid-January

By 25 Oct · landing pageHeadline "The habit tracker that lets go". A 15-second loop: a ring fills by itself, the bird hops out and flies. Waitlist. A short "Why your habit app should want you to leave" piece citing Lally and Renfree.
Oct–Nov · build in public, twice a weekThree content pillars: the science of graduation, design (the bird, the share card), and honest numbers. The research angle is more shareable than a feature list.
~15 Nov · Apple featuring nominationAngle: a native, private habit app built to make itself unnecessary. Mention accessibility (AX5, VoiceOver), iPad, widgets and Liquid Glass.
15 Nov – 10 Dec · creatorsBrief for 30 creators: show the ring filling from a walk, then the graduation share card. Hook: "the habit app that wants you to need it less". Free lifetime Pro codes.
23 Nov · public TestFlightThe first 100 waitlist users. Ask them one question: what did you graduate, or what nearly flew?
1 Dec · press kitA founder note on why the app retires habits, the research links, the share card, and the 13" and 6.9" screenshot sets.
15 Dec · launch; 26 Dec – 10 Jan · New YearThe In-app Event and offer. From late January, the first real graduations get shared as #FledgeFlew: the product markets itself once habits start flying.
5 Jan · Product HuntTagline: "The habit tracker that lets go."

8 · How we'll know

Measure the differentiator, not just downloads

  • Head start reach: the share of new Health habits that show a baseline, and how often people accept the suggested goal.
  • Graduations: the first ones can't happen before mid-January. Track the share of 30-day-old habits that are ready to fly.
  • Shares of the graduation card per graduation. This is the free growth channel.
  • Holding after flight: the share of flown habits still at or above 80% after 30 days. That is the proof for the next campaign.
  • Analytics are still undecided (ANA-1). These measures need anonymous counts only, and never Health values.
