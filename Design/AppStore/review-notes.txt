Thank you for reviewing Yearling.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE THE LAST REVIEW (guidelines 4.3 and 4.2.6): this version is a different product. It was a general habit tracker ("Fledge"); it is now Yearling, a New Year's resolution app. Yearling turns one resolution into at most three small habits, each with an if-then plan ("After lunch: walk"), fills them in from on-device Apple Health data, and lets each habit graduate once it sticks; when all have graduated, the resolution is kept. Our answers to your nine questions are in our reply in App Review.

NOT A TEMPLATE: written for this app only; no third-party SDKs or packages; no code shared with our other apps.

NO ACCOUNT: no sign-in, server or analytics. All data stays on the device.

HOW TO TEST (about 3 minutes, no Health data needed):
1. Onboarding, step 1: type "Stop scrolling in bed". A note suggests a "start" version; tap "Use Evenings off the phone" (or pick any resolution).
2. Step 2: suggested habits are pre-selected; also select "Read" (tap to log).
3. Step 3: each habit has an "After..." plan, prefilled and editable.
4. Step 4: a note to December (optional, "Skip for now").
5. Connect Apple Health if asked (or "Not now"), then reminders, then "Your plan": press and hold "Commit to my resolution".
6. Today: the resolution card shows the first week; tap a habit's ring to mark it done; long-press a card for Rest day or Pause.
7. Tap a habit for its detail (journey to fly, heatmap). Settings: gear icon.

APPLE HEALTH (READ-ONLY): access is requested only when a Health-based habit is added (steps, exercise minutes, sleep, mindful minutes, water, daylight, stand hours, workouts). Yearling reads daily totals to complete habits and never writes to Health. Health data is never sent anywhere or stored in iCloud. Without Health data, habits can be marked by hand, so every feature works on a review device.

IN-APP PURCHASES (Yearling Pro): free includes the resolution, three habits and one Health habit. Pro lets all three fill in from Health and adds every widget size, Lock Screen widgets, app icons and CSV export. It never unlocks more than three habits.
